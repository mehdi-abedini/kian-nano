## Overview

Kian Nano Karno is a scientific-technology organization operating between nanotechnology, biomedical engineering, industrial R&D, instrumentation, evidence-driven research, and technology deployment.

The visual language is precise, engineered, research-literate, restrained, and original. It should communicate that scientific reasoning can become an engineered and validated technology.

The design supports Persian RTL and English LTR as equal first-class modes.
## Colors

Use ink for primary text and high-contrast navigation. Use paper as the scientific canvas and surface for content containers. Signal is reserved for primary actions. Science marks research and validation pathways. Warning is for caution or unresolved status. Line provides quiet structure.

Avoid turning the palette into a conventional blue technology theme. Color encodes meaning rather than spectacle.

## Typography

Typography is part of the KKN fingerprint. Use fonts with robust Persian coverage. Do not substitute a Latin-only display font for Persian content.

Use display and heading roles for narrative hierarchy, body typography for technical reading, and monospace only for data, identifiers, methods, measurements, code, or technical metadata.

## Layout

Use a modular scientific grid with deliberate asymmetry where it improves hierarchy. Desktop pages use generous margins and controlled reading widths. Mobile preserves hierarchy rather than merely stacking cards.

RTL layouts mirror directional relationships while scientific notation, identifiers, URLs, code, and Latin technical terms retain correct bidi behavior.
## Elevation & Depth

Prefer borders, tonal surfaces, restrained shadows, and spatial separation over heavy glass or floating-card effects. Depth communicates information hierarchy and interaction state.

## Shapes

Use moderate radii. Sharp corners may serve technical/data surfaces; larger radii are reserved for primary interactive or narrative modules.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Make evidence and engineering logic visible.
- Use scientific metaphors only when they improve comprehension.
- Keep motion purposeful and low-amplitude.
- Treat citations and references as interface elements.
- Design for long technical reading.
- Distinguish research, prototype, product, and hypothesis.

### Don't
- Copy another company's signature layout, palette, typography, or animation language.
- Use AI-generated scientific imagery as experimental evidence.
- Optimize for novelty at the expense of readability.
- Hide technical context behind decorative interactions.
- Allow external reference refreshes to mutate this file automatically.
