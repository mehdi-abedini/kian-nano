# Drug Delivery & Drug Design — Research References

## Benchmarking
TDC ADMET Group: 22 public ADMET datasets with defined benchmark evaluation. Scaffold splitting is supported and is more structurally challenging than random splitting.

## Cheminformatics
RDKit provides molecular structures, descriptors, fingerprints and scaffold-related operations. Its public repository states a BSD license.
Chemprop provides message-passing neural networks for molecular property prediction under the MIT license.

## Governance
Benchmark scores are not therapeutic evidence. Predictions require provenance, split/leakage controls, uncertainty, applicability domain and external validation before translational interpretation.
