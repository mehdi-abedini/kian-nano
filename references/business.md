# Business, Market Intelligence, and Commercialization

## Scope
Use for market research, technology foresight, commercialization, licensing, JV analysis, procurement, competitive intelligence, and strategy support.

## Required structure
- Market definition and geography
- Customer/problem definition
- Alternatives and substitutes
- Technical and regulatory barriers
- Evidence for demand
- Competitive landscape
- Economics and deployment assumptions
- Risks and unknowns
- Falsifiable next steps

## Decision discipline
Do not turn a market estimate into a fact. Record source, date, methodology, and uncertainty.
