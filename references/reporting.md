# Research Reporting Contract

Every substantive report should contain:

1. Executive summary
2. Objective and scope
3. Assumptions
4. Search/research methodology
5. Evidence ledger
6. Findings
7. Competing interpretations
8. Quantitative analysis and calculations
9. Uncertainty and confidence
10. Limitations and missing evidence
11. Reproducibility information
12. Recommended experiments or actions
13. References

Use explicit labels:
- FACT
- CALCULATION
- INTERPRETATION
- HYPOTHESIS
- SPECULATION

For high-impact decisions, include an approval gate before external publication or irreversible action.
