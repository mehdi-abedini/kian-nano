# Science and Biomedical Research

## Scope
Use for nanoscience, materials, drug discovery, drug delivery, protein engineering, bioinformatics, genomics, biomedical engineering, clinical research, and translational R&D.

## Evidence hierarchy
1. Primary peer-reviewed research and validated datasets.
2. Official databases, standards, regulatory sources, and technical documentation.
3. Systematic reviews and authoritative secondary literature.
4. Preprints and expert commentary, clearly labelled.

## Required checks
- Research question and population/system definition.
- Experimental design, controls, sample size, statistics, and endpoint definitions.
- Replication and external validity.
- Units, assay methods, model assumptions, and data provenance.
- Whether a claim is mechanistic, correlational, predictive, or descriptive.

## Translational gate
Do not convert an in-vitro, computational, or animal result into a clinical or industrial claim without explicitly identifying missing validation stages.
