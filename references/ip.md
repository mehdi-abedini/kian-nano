# IP and Technology Intelligence

## Scope
Use for patents, prior-art discovery, freedom-to-operate research, technology landscaping, competitive intelligence, standards, and technology readiness.

## Workflow
1. Define the claim, technology, jurisdiction, and relevant dates.
2. Search patents, publications, standards, and product documentation.
3. Record publication, priority, filing, grant, assignee, inventor, and family information.
4. Map relevant disclosures to the technical elements under review.
5. Separate novelty evidence from infringement/FTO conclusions.
6. Flag legal questions for qualified patent counsel.

## Confidentiality
Do not place unpublished formulations, trade secrets, invention disclosures, or confidential contracts into a public repository or public report.
