# Industrial Engineering and Scale-Up

## Scope
Use for reactors, process equipment, instrumentation, process optimization, energy, petroleum, wastewater, nanomaterials, manufacturing, QC, and deployment.

## Workflow
1. Define feedstocks, products, operating envelope, constraints, and target KPIs.
2. Establish mass and energy balances where applicable.
3. Identify critical process parameters and critical quality attributes.
4. Design instrumentation and data acquisition around the control objective.
5. Evaluate laboratory, pilot, and production scale separately.
6. Estimate uncertainty, failure modes, maintenance, and operator requirements.
7. Assess cost, supply chain, safety, environmental, and regulatory constraints.
8. Define acceptance tests before recommending deployment.

## Scale-up rule
A successful laboratory formulation is evidence for feasibility, not proof of production robustness. Record scale, mixing regime, residence time, heat/mass transfer, and raw-material variability.
